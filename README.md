# Favorite K-Drama: Twinkling Watermelon 

> *"Viva La Vida!"* — A heartwarming time-travel youth drama about music, family, and second chances.

## Overview
**Twinkling Watermelon** (*반짝이는 워터멜론*) is a 2023 South Korean television series that follows **Ha Eun-gyeol**, a CODA (Child of Deaf Adults) student with a natural gift for music. After an argument with his father, Eun-gyeol mysteriously travels back in time to **1995**, where he meets the teenage version of his father, **Ha Yi-chan**, and helps form a band called *Watermelon Sugar*.

## Why It's My Favorite
* **Emotional Storytelling:** Balances comedy and deep family drama effortlessly.
* **Incredible Soundtrack:** Features unforgettable band performances and nostalgic 90s vibes.
* **Meaningful Representation:** Highlights sign language and the bond between hearing and deaf family members.
